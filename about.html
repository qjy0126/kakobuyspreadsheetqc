    About

    kakobuyspreadsheet is an independent Kakobuy Spreadsheet on kakobuyspreadsheetqc.com.

    We turn curated Taobao and Weidian finds into a fast, filterable catalog — with product images, category filters, Best Versions by brand, and direct Buy on Kakobuy links.

    This website is a discovery layer only. We do not process payments, hold inventory, or ship parcels. Checkout, warehouse Quality Check (QC) photos, and international shipping happen on Kakobuy.

    How the catalog works

    • Listings are sourced from community spreadsheet finds and seller pages on Weidian / Taobao.
    • Each card links out to Kakobuy so you can purchase through a shopping agent.
    • QC photos help you inspect items at the warehouse before you ship internationally.
    • The catalog is updated regularly as new finds are added.
